She left her house’s halls to become a traveling cleric, holding to the belief that a person’s deeds, not their pedigree, decide their worth.

He picked up sword and shield in earnest after that, deciding that a single gate was too small a thing to guard when there was a whole road full of people who needed the same promise kept.
